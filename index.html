Micro Modular notes

Same patch format

The Micro reads the same .pch “Nord Modular patch 3.0” files as the full G1. Any archive here applies.

One DSP slot

The full G1 has four slots and the Micro has one. Rack-sized patches can go over 100% load and refuse to run.

Three knobs

Knob assignments beyond three are lost. Re-map the important parameters to knobs 1–3, or to MIDI CC.

Zip before sending

Forum veterans warn that .pch files get mangled in mail and upload forms. Zip them first.
